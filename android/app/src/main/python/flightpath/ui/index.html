      Camera

      The app needs the GoPro on its WiFi and in the one video mode that works for this: 1080p at 240 frames per second, Linear lens, stabilisation off.

      Enginechecking Connectionchecking Resolution? Frame rate? Lens? HyperSmooth?

      1. On the camera: swipe down, Preferences, Connections, Wireless Connections: On. Leave the camera on its normal shooting screen. The very first time only, also go to Connections, Connect Device, GoPro App so the camera will pair with this phone, and accept the pairing request when it appears. After that the camera is remembered.

      2. On the phone: tap the green button below. A GoPro keeps its WiFi off until an app asks over Bluetooth, so the app does that, reads the camera's own network name and password, and joins it. No GoPro app is needed. When Android says the network has no internet, tap keep connected.

      Do it by hand instead

      The camera's WiFi has to be on already for this path (the button above does that for you). The name and password are on the camera under Preferences, Connections, Camera Info. The phone's WiFi and Location toggles must be on; Android hides networks otherwise.

      Only this app joins the camera. Your phone stays on 5G for everything else, so calls and messages keep working.

      Open WiFi settings

      Samsung: turn off Intelligent Wi-Fi › Switch to mobile data, or it will kick you off the camera.

      Position the camera

      Side-on is what makes the maths simple. Get these five right and the numbers take care of themselves.

      1. Tripod 8 to 10 ft directly to your side, lens pointing straight across the target line, not angled toward or away from the target. Every degree off makes the ball read slow.
      2. Lens low, between ball height and knee height.
      3. 6 to 8 ft of clear space in front of the ball in the frame, so it stays in view for several frames.
      4. Lay a club on the ground where the ball will fly, parallel to the camera, at the same distance from the lens as the ball. That is your scale in the next step.
      5. Daylight, sun behind or beside the camera. Not into the lens.
      See what the camera sees while you move the tripod.

      Calibrate

      The app measures speed in pixels. Tapping both ends of the club tells it how many pixels make a metre.

      Tap the first end of the club.
